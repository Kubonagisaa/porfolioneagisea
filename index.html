03 / About

The designer behind it

NeaGiSea

Design is a series of decisions — I just make sure each one has a reason.

I come from hands-on building: I design and I understand how the code beneath works. That means no mockups that can't ship, no handoff surprises, and spacing that survives real content.

I care about typography first. A page wins or loses on its type rhythm long before any color or effect matters. Then hierarchy. Then restraint — the discipline to remove the third accent color, the fourth animation, the decorative element doing nobody any favors.

When you hire me, you get a designer who treats your project like a portfolio piece — because it becomes one. I ask about your audience before your preferences, argue for the user when needed, and deliver work you can defend in a room.

How a project runs

4 steps · 2–6 weeks

Brief

We define the audience, the single goal of the page, and what "done" looks like. I ask the uncomfortable questions early.

Week 1 · Async + call
02

Direction

Typography, color and layout decisions as a visible design direction. You react to a real page, not a mood board of borrowed vibes.

Week 1–2 · 1 round
03

Design

The full page takes shape: composition, states, responsive behavior. Reviews at fixed checkpoints, so feedback never piles up.

Week 2–4 · 2 rounds
04

Deliver

Final files, organized and documented — ready for your developer, or built by me if that's the agreement. Nothing vanishes after sign-off.
